Mitengo Privacy Policy

Last updated: [DATE]

1. Who we are

Mitengo is run by RJM Web Technologies, [BUSINESS ADDRESS], Lusaka, Zambia. Contact us at [YOUR CONTACT EMAIL].

2. What we collect

  • Account: your email address, your password (stored in scrambled form by our login provider), and the date you accepted our Terms.
  • Receipts: photos you upload, the store you choose, and the upload time.
  • Scouts: the prices you record and your user ID.
  • Reports: wrong-price reports you send.
  • Plans: which plan you chose and its payment reference.
  • On your device only: your basket, watchlist and mode. We do not receive these.
  • Basic technical logs kept by our hosting providers, such as IP address and device type.

We do not collect your location, contacts or card numbers.

3. Why we use it

To run your account and plan, check receipts and prevent fraud, keep prices accurate, answer you, and meet legal duties. We rely on your consent and on what we need to provide the service.

4. Receipts

Upload only your own receipts. Cover or crop names, phone numbers and card digits first. Our staff may view receipts to check them. We keep receipt photos for up to [12] months, or until you delete your account, whichever is sooner.

5. Sharing

We do not sell your data. We use service providers to run Mitengo: Supabase (accounts, database, file storage) and Vercel (hosting) [and your payment provider, if added]. We share data when the law requires it. Prices you help record may appear in the app without your name.

6. Where data is kept

Our providers may store data outside Zambia. We take steps to keep it protected as the law requires.

7. Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, and withdraw consent. You can delete your account in the app under Account, then Delete my account, which also removes your receipts. You can complain to Zambia's Office of the Data Protection Commissioner.

8. Security

We use encrypted connections, access controls and private file storage, and only approved people review receipts. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects you, we will tell you and the regulator as the law requires.

9. Children

Mitengo is for people aged 18 and over.

10. Changes

We will update this page when things change and show the new date at the top.
